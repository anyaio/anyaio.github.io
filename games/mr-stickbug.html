mr.stickbug

Roll the acorn up to the hungry squirrel on the high plateau. It's fully driven by physics, so push it along and let the hills help. Watch out for holes: if the acorn drops in, climb down after it and push it up the ramp on the far side. Big mushroom caps bounce the acorn. Worms pop out of the ground to block the way: hop on top of one to push it back down. Watch out for ants: they steal the acorn, and if one carries it all the way back to the start, it's game over. Jump on them to defeat them and get it back. At streams, dive in and push it across the water.

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a bigger view.
